# Review checklist

Review the preview, not the script. Extract frames at every anchor and a contact sheet of the whole video.

## Story
- [ ] Hook understandable with the sound off (picture + on-screen text) and with the picture off (line alone).
- [ ] The one sentence the viewer must understand is actually said and shown.
- [ ] Problem and consequence are visible; fix is installed; retest repeats the same failure test.
- [ ] Every open loop is paid off; every payoff was planted.
- [ ] Anticipation (pause/silence) before each strong moment.
- [ ] Long-form: rhythm changes between blocks; no block longer than ~90 s without a change of look or pace.

## Picture
- [ ] At each anchor, the frame shows the action of the sentence (if not, it is a defect even if the render finished without errors).
- [ ] Camera lets you read the action; no motion that hides it.
- [ ] Labels point to visible, installed parts; subtitles at the bottom; one text at a time.
- [ ] Hands and objects in recognizable proportion; character continuity marks consistent.
- [ ] No invented people, wrong text or wrong numbers generated inside the image.
- [ ] No long static stretches.

## Sound
- [ ] Duration, loudness and peaks measured.
- [ ] Music never covers the voice.
- [ ] Items that need a human listen are listed (pronunciation, word endings, technical terms). Never claim you listened.

## Delivery
- [ ] Facts table complete; disputed facts flagged.
- [ ] Voice is the creator's own or authorized; music and effects licensed.
- [ ] No keys, private files or personal data in the shared package.
- [ ] Title and thumbnail represent the video; no invented metrics.
- [ ] Publication only after explicit approval.

Report defects as: **timestamp — sentence — what is wrong — fix**. Separate measured results from editorial opinion. After fixes, re-check only the changed segments, and keep approved audio when the fix is visual only.
